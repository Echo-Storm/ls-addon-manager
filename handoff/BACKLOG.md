## Single-frame neural upscaling for the static look (issue #8), researched 2026-10-02

- **What the reporter wants** is NVIDIA's RTX Video Super Resolution (VSR), a trained single-frame network that invents edge detail; our temporal upscalers cannot without jitter (measured; answered on the issue, "Sharpness at rest" 0.3 to 0.5 is the setting that helps).
- **What it takes, found by search:** the RTX Video SDK (https://developer.nvidia.com/rtx-video-sdk). The download needs a free NVIDIA developer sign-in and acceptance of the RTX Video SDK licence; its components (`nvngx_vsr.dll`, `nvngx_truehdr.dll`) are NVIDIA's and cannot be redistributed. So the only route is the one the Neural Rendering model uses: **the person downloads it themselves** and the addon loads it from a folder. Nothing of NVIDIA's is shipped or fetched by us.
- **What we have:** `external/ngx/include/nvsdk_ngx_defs.h` only defines the availability flags (`NVSDK_NGX_Feature_VideoSuperResolution = 5`, `VideoSuperResolution.Available` and the driver-version keys). The create and run calls, and the model, are in the RTX Video SDK itself.
- **Measured 2026-10-02 (the owner installed the Video Effects SDK 1.3.0.0 x64 and its VideoSuperRes feature in `external/vfx_x64`; `nr_vfxprobe`, tools/vfx_probe.cpp):** VSR modes Low to Ultra are the RTX VSR itself (`nvngx_vsr.dll`, modes 1-4, plus 8-19 denoise / deblur / high bitrate, 21 and 23 streaming). GPU time per frame on the 4070 Ti SUPER: 2560x1440 to 3840x2160: bicubic 0.9 ms, Low 2.3, Medium 2.9, High 6.4, Ultra 8.4; 1708x961 to 2562x1442: Low 1.4, Medium 3.5, High 13, Ultra 20. Detail (mean edge strength, truth = 100 %): bicubic 65-78 %, VSR Low 82-93 %, higher modes no better than Low. PSNR the same as bicubic (it sharpens, it does not match pixels). Steadiness over 40 consecutive frames of the owner's moving WoW clip (the input a 1.5x box shrink of each frame): the result's change where the game's picture did not change (still, levels of 255) bicubic 1.65, Low 2.11 (+28 %), Medium/High/Ultra 2.46-2.49 (+50 %); the error of the result's change against the truth's (moving) 3.16, 3.97 (+25 %), 4.3-4.5. Our DLSS upscaler on the same frames by `nr_sreval` (a different flicker measure, against its own plain stretch): flicker 99 % of the stretch, detail 110 %. So VSR Low takes about twice the detail gain of the DLSS pass for about 25-30 % more frame-to-frame change (under 1 % of the range); Medium to Ultra only shimmer more. Low is the mode. Not measured: a real lower-resolution render (the input is a shrink of the truth), HDR (VSR takes 8-bit or RGB10A2), the cost of getting a D3D frame in and out of CUDA (the SDK works on CUDA buffers, with D3D transfer helpers in nvTransferD3D11.h), and a live look.
- **Hand-over measured 2026-10-02 (`nr_vfxprobe handover=1`, a D3D11 device and BGRA8 textures, the SDK's own `NvCVImage_Transfer` between them and the CUDA buffers the effect works on):** 2560x1440 to 3840x2160, VSR Low: D3D11 to CUDA 0.29 ms, the run 2.34 ms, CUDA to D3D11 0.37 ms; the three queued together 2.98 ms, so the hand-over is about 0.65 ms (22 % on top of the run). Still to add in a real addon: our own conversion of the frame to BGRA8 (the SDR view for HDR frames) and back (the place pass), each a compute pass of a few tenths of a millisecond at 4K; so about 3.5 to 4 ms per upscaled frame at 4K. **That is per presented frame:** with frame generation x3 on a 39 fps game, upscaling every presented frame is three runs per real frame, about 10 to 12 ms of a 25.6 ms frame; the existing "Lighter upscaling of generated frames" idea (the real frame upscaled, the generated ones cheaper) would be the way to keep it affordable. Not measured: the same inside Lossless Scaling's process, on its own device (the textures would be ours, made on its device).
- **WoW interface measured 2026-10-02 (`nr_vfxprobe seq= roi=`, the owner's WoW clip of 30 September, 40 frames, input a 1.5x shrink; VSR ran on the whole frame, the scores are of the region):** quest tracker (text) bicubic / Low / High: shimmer where the picture is still 4.92 / 6.28 / 6.77, detail kept 77 / 91 / 85 %; chat 4.78 / 5.82 / 6.72, 82 / 97 / 93 %; action bars (icons) 0.78 / 1.38 / 2.03, 87 / 106 / 104 %. By eye (crops of the middle frame at 3x): VSR Low renders the quest text, the chat and the action bar icons crisp and close to what the game drew, with no halos or ringing seen; High looks about the same. So Low is the mode for the UI too; the cost is about 22-27 % more shimmer on text (a few levels of 255 on a still picture) and a slight over-sharpening of icons (106 %). Untested: text that scrolls (chat, floating combat text), and nameplates moving over the world.
- **Owner's decision, 2026-10-02: the VSR test addon ships in the package with the NVIDIA Video Effects SDK files it needs** (`addons/VSRUPSC/vfx/`: `NVCVImage`, `NVVideoEffects`, `nvngxruntime`, `cudart64_12`, `libcrypto-3-x64`, `nvngx_vsr`, `nvVFXVideoSuperRes` and the SDK's licence documents, about 66 MB of the SDK's 1.6 GB, found by removing files until `nr_vfxprobe` and the host scenarios stopped working; the `npp*` and cuBLAS/TensorRT libraries are not needed). "We can strip later; a download option for everything legal comes later." **Open: the legal review of redistributing those files (NVIDIA's Software License Agreement and product terms, `external/vfx_x64/license/`) before anything past a test release; the download option is the way out.** NOTICE.md says so.
- **If it is built:** a separate addon, runtime from the user's folder (never shipped; NVIDIA's licence and models), VSR Low by default, optional use only where the picture is still (a motion gate from LSFG's flow, as the steady passes do), SDR view for HDR. First step: the D3D11 to CUDA hand-over cost on a 4K frame.

## Fresh-eyes review 2026-10-09: found, NOT fixed (two independent reviewers, plus mine)

Fixed in 3400db0 (see its message). Left, with why (each is real, none is a crash seen in use; all need a design choice or a live test):
- **Manager, `AddonManager::m_mutex` is held across addon code** (mitigated since: `InterceptResource` waits for it at most 50 ms and then lets Lossless Scaling have its own resource, so a modal dialog in an addon's panel no longer stalls it for long; the refactor itself is still undone) (`RenderAddonSettings`, `AddonInitialize/Shutdown`), and `InterceptResource` (LS's `FindResourceW`) takes the same lock: an addon panel that opens a modal dialog stalls Lossless Scaling's next resource lookup. Fix = copy what is needed under the lock and call addon code outside it. Not done: a refactor of the addon manager's locking without a live test.
- **Shutdown assumptions** (`core/main.cpp`: loader lock, GUI thread killed at `ExitProcess` while holding a lock; hooks left in place on `FreeLibrary`). Depends on how Lossless Scaling exits.
- **Fixed afterwards (2026-10-09, uncompiled until the matrix ends):** the event bus now drains publishes in flight before Unsubscribe/ForgetCode return; a dispatch callback that changes its own hooks is refused and logged (ihost.h says so); `dll`, `icon` and `runtimes[].file` in addon.json that are absolute, name a drive or climb with `..` are dropped.
- **Addon security level is advisory**: the verdict is not re-checked at load, only the one DLL is hashed, `trusted_addons.json` is in the writable folder. Import of a settings file can set `security_level` and `_enabled`. Raises no new risk beyond "can write the addons folder", but the manifest paths should be refused.
- **D3D11 device/context pointers handed to addons** are raw and unsynchronised; `g_lsContexts` keeps contexts for ever.
- **`NrEngine::EndBuild` waits INFINITE** for the model build thread while the caller holds `g_frameMutex`: a model change during a build freezes Lossless Scaling until it ends (bounded by the build's GPU wait; unbounded if NGX hangs).
- **`Tappable` accepts any previously seen NVIDIA device**, not only `g_tapDevice`: with two NVIDIA devices dispatching, `Bridge::Init(g_tapDevice, ctx)` could pair a device with another's context. Dual-GPU; needs a dual-GPU machine to change safely.
- **Recorder**: `Collect(true)` blocks the render thread up to 4 s on a size/format change or when switched off; after its timeout `DropStaging` can unmap memory a worker still reads. Rare (recorder on + a change). **Fixed 2026-10-20 (`Forget` joins the workers first when the wait timed out);** `Collect(true)` still blocks the render thread up to 4 s.
- **Cadence probe**: runs once per loaded addon (up to four compute passes per captured frame with all addons on; add a process-wide owner like `kOwnerVariable`); it makes its own SRV of slice 0 of the frame (blind if the frames are array slices).
- **Per-dispatch cost**: `FindNisPass` (scalers) reads the bindings again after `FrameTap::Bound` did; one read could serve both.
- **NIS viewport cache** (`nis_pass.cpp`) is never re-read for the same pass key if LS changes its output viewport without changing the key.
- **Fixed 2026-10-20: the updater refuses a zip GitHub lists no SHA-256 for** (update_test covers it; the release page's own download is the way then). It had accepted one (the old text: "still accepts a zip with no GitHub digest (now said in the offer and the Setup is re-hashed before it runs); a release without a digest should probably be refused.
- **Fixed 2026-10-09:** config.json (and an imported settings backup) nested more than 64 deep is treated as damaged (kept as config.json.corrupt) instead of overflowing the stack in `dump`; `WriteAtomically` writes the temporary file through and flushes it to the disk before the swap (core_test covers both). Also already done and listed above by mistake: the `NrEngine::EndBuild` wait is bounded (20 s) and the cadence probe has a process-wide owner (`EAM_SOURCE_CADENCE`).

## The recorder as a feature of the manager (2026-10-19; board M1, done offline, not yet seen live)

- **Shape:** `manager/src/features/recorder_service.{h,cpp}` (no ImGui, no config: the recorder, a FrameTap per Lossless Scaling context to find the captured frame, the hotkey, the note, the beep, the clip), `recorder_feature.{h,cpp}` (config section "Recorder", the poll thread, the Features-tab options, the hooks). It is compiled from the addons' sources (`addon/recorder.cpp`, `lsrec.cpp`, `frame_tap.cpp`: `EAM_ADDONS_SRC` in manager/CMakeLists.txt) and by the Neural Rendering host test, whose fake host now runs the real service. The manager's own dispatch hook calls it (`D3D11Hook::SetDispatchObserver`).
- **API 1.3:** `RecorderFlags`, `RecorderOffer`, `RecorderSave`, event `EAM_EVENT_RECORDING_SAVED`. The addons' `min_host_version` is 1.3.0 (VSR's stays 1.2.0: it records nothing).
- **Source rules:** the manager's tap records the captured frames (frame generation on) and ignores offers of that source; addons offer presented frames (frame generation off: Neural Rendering's present mode; "record what is shown") and NIS's input; the service accepts a presented offer when "what is shown" is on or no captured frame came in the last 1.5 s. With "what is shown" on the tap is off.
- **Frame generation OFF, no addon on (found live the same evening: the owner's first test had both):** the service falls back to the input of Lossless Scaling's scaling pass (`FindNisPass`, from the addons' `nis_pass.cpp`), recorded as the captured frame, once a context has shown no TAP for 3 s and the tap offers nothing; matrix scenario `record_fg_off`. Only works while Lossless Scaling scales with NIS (the pass is recognised by its shape); other scaling types record nothing, and a press that finds nothing says so (log + low beep).
- **Compact / Exact (2026-10-19, after the first live HDR clip: 8.5 GB for 10 s of 2560x1440 scRGB, 48 s to write):** `Recorder::SetCompact`: a frame of more than 8 bits goes through a compute pass on the card (a deferred context executed with the immediate context's state restored, so Lossless Scaling's own pass is not disturbed) into an 8-bit RGBA picture (scRGB scaled by 80/SDR white, HDR10 PQ to nits/SDR white, sRGB curve; highlights above the SDR white clip), recorded as content SDR view; 8-bit frames are unchanged. Default Compact (config `compact`), Exact is the old lossless half-float. The note beside a file says which. Not measured live yet: the real size and the cost of the pass at 4K.
- **Known gaps / ideas:** a video export after a save (ffmpeg is on the PATH now: `nr_lsrec video`), a size cap on the recordings folder, the manager's UI preview with the Recording options. Not touched: the `.lsrec` format and the tools.
